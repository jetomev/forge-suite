# Screenshots (applet 8)

**What it does:** takes a picture of the screen, saves it, copies it so you can paste it
straight away, and lets you draw on it.

## How to use it

| Keys | What it takes |
|---|---|
| **Print Screen** | Drag a box around what you want (**Esc** cancels) |
| **Shift + Print Screen** | The screen you are on |
| **Ctrl + Print Screen** | Every screen |
| **Alt + Print Screen** | The window you are in |

Every screenshot is **saved** in `Pictures/Screenshots` (the date and time in its name),
**copied** — paste it anywhere with **Ctrl + V** — and announced by a notification.
**Click the notification** to open the picture in **swappy** and draw on it: pen, text, boxes,
circles, arrows, and **blur** to hide something. **Ctrl + S** saves the drawn copy next to the
original; **Ctrl + C** copies it. swappy cannot crop: the box you drag is the crop.

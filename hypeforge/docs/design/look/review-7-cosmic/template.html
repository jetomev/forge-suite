COSMIC, tiled: a clean top panel, the dock, the App Library, and the active hint

System76's COSMIC is the closest of the six to what hypeForge already is: a desktop that tiles. Its look on our pieces, with no COSMIC software: a top panel with Workspaces and Applications on the left, the clock in the middle and small applets on the right; a dock at the bottom; the App Library with your groups as folders; and the active hint, a frame in the theme's colour around the window you're in. COSMIC lets you pick how round everything is, so this page does too.

What I need from you

Five calls, and anything that feels off

My pick is marked on each. The last proposal: after this, the build.

C-1 · Panel and dock

Both, like COSMIC's default?

The top panel always; the dock at the bottom with the App Library button, your favourites and what's running. Or the top panel alone, windows using the full height. Try "Dock".

my pick: both

C-2 · Roundness

Which of COSMIC's three, by default?

Round (16 px, pill buttons), Slightly round (8 px) or Square (0). All three would be a choice in hypeForge Settings; Square is the one plain Sway can draw for windows. Try "Roundness".

my pick: slightly round

C-3 · The active hint

A frame around the window you're in?

COSMIC's tiling hint: a 3 px frame in the theme's bright colour, a pixel outside the window, so you always know where you are across three screens. Try "Active hint".

my pick: yes

C-4 · Finding apps

The launcher and the App Library, both?

Win opens the launcher: a search box near the top with results and their keys (today's launcher, restyled). The dock's first button opens the App Library: every app as a grid, your groups as folders under it (our panel, shared with Launchpad).

my pick: both

C-5 · Applets

Each applet its own small pop-up?

COSMIC's way: sound, network, Bluetooth, notifications each open their own pop-up from their icon. (KDE's way is one tray pop-up for all.)

my pick: each its own

Honest limits

What this COSMIC won't have

Not on Sway

  • COSMIC's workspace overview with live window pictures; the Workspaces strip shows which apps are where.
  • Window stacks with tabs drawn by the desktop in COSMIC's shape; Sway's tabs (Win + T) take their place.
  • The tiling toggle per workspace exactly as COSMIC does it: our Placement applet always tiles, with floating one key away.

Different on purpose

  • No COSMIC software (D-56): its look, hypeForge's pieces.
  • The App Library opens on your groups, and every app still opens in its own workspace.
  • No animations (SwayFX #565 and #569).

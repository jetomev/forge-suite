The Linux rice: floating islands, big gaps, see-through terminals, a mood on the wall

"Ricing" is what the Linux community calls dressing a desktop up by hand, the setups people show off on r/unixporn. This is our take: the bar breaks into three floating islands, workspaces are pills, terminals let the wallpaper glow through, and every theme brings its own generated wallpaper. It is the most terminal-proud of the six, so it fits the Forge Suite's philosophy best. Pick a theme and watch the whole mood change.

Honest limits

What this rice won't have

Not on Sway

  • Gradient window frames (one colour per state only).
  • Animated windows sliding and popping in: SwayFX's animations stay off (#565, #569).
  • Hyprland-only tricks people show off (wobbly windows, shaders on the screen).

Different on purpose

  • Every piece is a hypeForge piece: the islands are our bar, the pills our Workspaces, the launcher ours.
  • Nothing from swaync, eww or other big add-ons: our rules keep the install small.

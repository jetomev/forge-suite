Honest risks

What could slow it down

Known risks

  • SwayFX on your Nvidia card is "not supported" by its makers (same as Sway); it may need driver settings or turn out slow on three 144 Hz screens. Plain Sway still gets every style.
  • SwayFX can freeze after several config reloads with shadows on (#566): the bench runs ten reloads before you ever see it.
  • Our own graphical panels are new territory (Python + GTK); the first one sets the pattern and may take longer.
  • KDE is still installed: a Plasma login can rewrite the GTK look again (F-52) until KDE leaves.

How the work stays safe

  • Every change to the desktop passes the hidden test desktop first (the F-51 rule).
  • Every theme switch keeps a backup and an undo; today's look stays one command away.
  • The SwayFX login sits beside the normal one; your Sway login is never touched.
  • Each step ends with your test; nothing stacks on an untested step.

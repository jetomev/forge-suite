KDE Plasma, tiled: the floating panel, Kickoff, the pager and one tray for everything

Plasma 6's look, built from hypeForge's own pieces and no KDE software (KognogOS ships Sway only). A floating panel at the bottom with Kickoff, a pager that shows your workspaces as little screens, your apps in the middle, and one tray pop-up that holds every setting. Windows get Breeze-style title bars. Dark Gray is the closest theme to Breeze Dark; Blue looks great too.

What I need from you

Five calls, and anything that feels off

My pick is marked on each. Tell me in your own words what doesn't feel like Plasma.

K-1 · The panel

Floating, or attached to the edge?

Plasma 6 floats its panel a few pixels off the bottom, with rounded ends. Attached runs it edge to edge, like Plasma 5. Try "Panel" above the screen.

my pick: floating

K-2 · Workspaces

The pager?

Your six workspaces as six little screens in the panel, the one you're on lit in the theme's colour, small marks where windows are. Click one to go there; hover for its name.

my pick: the pager

K-3 · Kickoff

KDE's list, with descriptions?

Categories on the left, apps as a list with what each one does under its name, you and the search on top, Applications/Places and the power buttons at the bottom. (Windows 11's Start is the grid version.)

my pick: the list

K-4 · Title bars

Breeze-style title bars?

A title bar on every window, title on the left, matching the window rather than the accent (Breeze's way). Each window gives up 28 px. Off means frames only. Try "Title bars".

my pick: on

K-5 · The tray

One tray pop-up for everything?

Plasma's way: one pop-up with every setting as an icon at the top (sound, network, Bluetooth, clipboard, drives, night light, notifications, printers), its page below. Instead of a separate pop-up for each.

my pick: one pop-up

Honest limits

What this Plasma won't have

Not on Sway

  • The app's icon and the minimize, maximize and close buttons in the title bar: Sway draws the title only.
  • The Overview (every window and workspace at once) and live window thumbnails on hover.
  • Desktop widgets (sticky notes, a clock on the wallpaper).
  • The panel turning solid by itself when a window is maximized.

Different on purpose

  • No KDE software at all: Plasma's look, hypeForge's pieces (D-56).
  • Windows tile; the pager is how you move between workspaces.
  • No animations (SwayFX #565 and #569).

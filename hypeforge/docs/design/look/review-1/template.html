What I need from you

Five answers

Each one has my pick marked. "Your pick is fine" is a perfectly good answer.

Q-1 · SwayFX

Can the rounded, shadowed looks use SwayFX?

Plain Sway can't round corners, cast shadows or blur. SwayFX is Sway with those added, built on the same Sway 1.12 we run, and everything we have works on it. It would be a second login, "hypeForge FX"; plain Sway stays the default and every style is drawn for plain Sway first. Our rule D-56 says "Sway only".

  • AYes, SwayFX counts as Sway, as a second loginmy pick
  • BNo, plain Sway only: flat looks
Q-2 · Graphical pop-ups

May the bar open small graphical panels?

A KDE-style launcher, Windows-style Quick Settings, a calendar. They need no new installs: everything is already on the machine. The Forge apps stay in the terminal exactly as they are. Our rule D-57 says terminal apps first.

  • AYes, small panels from the bar; Forge apps stay terminalmy pick
  • BNo, keep today's text lists (fuzzel) for everything
Q-3 · Wallpapers

Our own pictures only?

Our generator draws them, so they are ours to ship. Photo sites (Unsplash, Pexels, Wallhaven) don't allow shipping their pictures inside a system. Free-licence art from Wikimedia (CC0 or Creative Commons) is possible one file at a time, with credits.

  • AOur generator onlymy pick
  • BOur generator, plus hand-picked free-licence art
Q-4 · The middle themes

Deeper or lighter middles?

Gray, Blue, Purple, Green, Pink, Red and Multicolor read close to their Dark versions today. Strict readability (text 7 times brighter than its background) forces them down. The lighter option keeps text at 4.5 times, the standard minimum. Flip "Middle themes" next to the desktop to compare.

  • ADeeper: the strictest readability
  • BLighter: clearly "middle", standard readabilitymy pick

What the research found

What the six styles can and can't have

Five research reports, all in docs/research/look-2026-10/. The short version, so the proposals don't promise what Sway can't do.

Yes, we can

  • Any bar layout: top or bottom, floating in the middle, a second bar as a dock, drawers that slide open, real drop-down menus. All with the Waybar we already run.
  • Rounded corners, shadows and blur on windows, bars and notifications, with SwayFX (Q-1).
  • A KDE-style launcher, Quick Settings and a calendar panel (Q-2), with nothing new to install.
  • Every program follows one theme file: Sway, the bar, the lists, notifications, the lock screen, GTK apps, the terminal.

No, Sway can't

  • An app's own File and Edit menus up in the top bar (Mac style).
  • The macOS dock's magnifying wave.
  • Minimizing windows (we can tuck them away instead).
  • A zoomed-out view of every workspace.
  • Animations: SwayFX has them, but they hide workspaces moved between screens, which we do all the time, so they stay off.

What the research found

What the six styles can and can't have

Five research reports, all in docs/research/look-2026-10/. The short version, so the proposals don't promise what Sway can't do.

Yes, we can

  • Any bar layout: top or bottom, floating in the middle, a second bar as a dock, drawers that slide open, real drop-down menus. All with the Waybar we already run.
  • Rounded corners, shadows and blur on windows, bars and notifications, with SwayFX (Q-1).
  • A KDE-style launcher, Quick Settings and a calendar panel (Q-2), with nothing new to install.
  • Every program follows one theme file: Sway, the bar, the lists, notifications, the lock screen, GTK apps, the terminal.

No, Sway can't

  • An app's own File and Edit menus up in the top bar (Mac style).
  • The macOS dock's magnifying wave.
  • Minimizing windows (we can tuck them away instead).
  • A zoomed-out view of every workspace.
  • Animations: SwayFX has them, but they hide workspaces moved between screens, which we do all the time, so they stay off.

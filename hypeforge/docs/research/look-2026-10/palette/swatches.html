Every theme is built the 60-30-10 way: a neutral foundation (windows, panels, the terminal), the theme colour as a strong band (the bar, panel headers, the side panel, the selected row, the focused border), and a pop in a harmony colour for small important things (the letters you typed, toggles, badges, buttons, the bell's dot). The strip under each name shows the split. Every text and border pair is measured with WCAG 2.2 and APCA.

# hypeForge — the look, piece by piece

*Every place the desktop's look is set: the file, the values in it today, and why. This is the blueprint for the **Theme manager** Forge app (D-59): each row is something it will set. Javier, 2026-10-05: "there is going to be a lot of notes about how we have changed the system. We need to document those, because they are part of the theme management app."*

**Since 2026-10-10 the look is applied by `hypeforge-theme`** (D-78, the look program's step 1): a **style** (`styles/<slug>/style.toml`: shapes and which colour role each part wears) plus a **theme** (`themes/colours/palettes.toml`: 25 colour themes, 60-30-10, D-70/D-73) fill the templates in `themes/templates/` and write `~/.config/hypeforge/theme/{sway.conf, colors.css, fuzzel-colors.ini}`, `~/.config/mako/config`, `~/.config/gtk-3.0|gtk-4.0/{gtk.css, settings.ini}`, the lock screen's blurred wallpaper, and **the terminal** (`~/.config/alacritty/themes/hypeForge-desktop.toml`, first in Alacritty's imports; alacrittyForge shows it locked; a theme picked in alacrittyForge wins — Javier 10-10: the terminals follow the theme); Sway, the bar, the lists and the lock screen *include* those files. **Everything below describes today's look = the "hypeForge Classic" style with the KognogOS Mocha theme**; the only differences from the hand-written values are Mocha's approved tiny changes (D-73 F-4: urgent and Do Not Disturb red `#ff99b5`, subtext `#bac2de`). Commands: `hypeforge-theme list | current | apply <style> <theme> [--dry-run] | wallpaper theme|<file> | undo`. **themeForge** (Forge Suite, 2026-10-11) is the app for it: it writes **the look file** `~/.config/hypeforge/look.toml` (theme, colors, `[elements]`; D-90) and runs `hypeforge-theme look`; each style's `[meta] summary` and `[elements]` are what it shows. **GTK apps already open repaint** (F-70): each look writes its own named GTK theme (`~/.local/share/themes/hypeForge-<style>-<theme>-<fingerprint>`, the palette's adw-gtk3 imported, then the look's colours) and names it in `settings.ini` and GNOME's setting; `~/.config/gtk-3.0/gtk.css` stays empty on purpose (it would outrank the theme and is read only at start). **Undo ends like an apply** (F-67): the bar restarted whole, never reloaded in place into another layout. **Readable in every theme** (F-68, `tests/test_contrast.py`, WCAG 4.5 for text, 3 for icons, every style × all 25 themes): a style that paints text on a background must take the text colour made for THAT background — the Rice's areas `surface.base` with `text.primary`; Classic's band `bar.bg` with `bar.fg` (its bar, title bars, GTK headers and its workspace square); macOS's Dock `surface.overlay` with `@hf_menubar_text`. Mocha is unchanged (the two text colours are the same there). Proven on the hidden bench first: `python3 scripts/theme-bench.py` (every style × theme through Sway's and fuzzel's own checks; three looks running on a screen-less Sway; wallpaper + undo). **Note:** `sway -C` exits 0 even when an *included* file has errors; read its "Error on line" output. **The SwayFX login** (D-68, step 2): SwayFX 0.6 (`fd71a6b`, built into `~/.local/opt/swayfx`, beside Sway) starts with `~/.local/opt/swayfx/bin/hypeforge-fx` → `sway/config-fx` = `sway/config` + `~/.config/hypeforge/theme/fx.conf`, which hypeforge-theme writes from the style's `[fx]` table (corner_radius, shadows, shadow_blur_radius/colour/offset, blur, blur_passes/radius, default_dim_inactive, `layer_effects` for waybar / launcher / notifications, `animation_duration_ms 0`). Plain Sway never reads an FX line. Bench: `python3 scripts/fx-bench.py` (every style through SwayFX's check; on the GPU with a window and shadows, 10 reloads — #566 did not bite, 10/10). **Classic's FX corners: 12 px** (Javier, 2026-10-10, after his first FX login: at 8 px the curve of the 2 px frame looked stair-stepped; a bench zoom of 8/10/12 px picked 12). Note for the other styles: Windows 11 and KDE still say 8 in their `[fx]` table — the same stair-step will show; decide when they are built. **Classic's bar casts no shadow in FX** (`bar_shadows = false`, 10-10): flush against the windows, its shadow dimmed their top frame and top corners (seen in Javier's screenshot, reproduced on a hidden SwayFX). A style whose bar floats away from the windows may keep it. **Known, not ours (F-56):** on big windows (a full-screen terminal) SwayFX 0.6 draws a small notch where the curved corner meets the straight frame; windows up to about half a screen are clean. **Since D-80 (Javier, 10-10, replacing D-79's no-frame): in FX, Classic draws a quiet 2 px frame** — the window in use `#585b70` (`border.subtle*2+border.strong`), the others `#313244` (`surface.overlay`) — with 10 px at the screen edges, the same as between windows (`gap_outer = 0`; 20 was too much, Javier) and the others dimmed 0.12; quiet colours hide most of F-56's notch. Plain Sway keeps today's frame and edges. Floating Forge apps still get their 3 px frame from the rules applet.

**Rule:** a change to the look updates this page in the same commit, like Help. Values here were read from the files on 2026-10-05, not written from memory. The files under `hypeforge/` are the source; each is copied to the path shown when it is applied.

### Corners (D-81, 2026-10-10)

`hypeforge-theme corners straight|rounded|style` (or `apply … --corners …`), saved with the look and kept through later changes: **straight** = every corner square (style shapes `radius_*` → 0, the FX login's `corner_radius 0`, every `border-radius` in the style's bar stylesheet → 0, the panels' round buttons and sliders square); **rounded** = a square style gets windows 12 · pop-ups 10 · bar areas 12 · inner areas 8 · controls 6 (its bar stylesheet keeps its own); **style** = the style's own (the default). `hypeforge-theme current` shows it. The lock screen's password box (22 px, `sway/gtklock/style.css`) doesn't follow yet.

### The bar follows the style (look program step 5, 2026-10-10 →)

`hypeforge-theme apply` now writes the bar too: each style's **layout** (`config.jsonc`: which pieces, where) and **shapes** (`style.css`) into `~/.config/sway/waybar/`, plus **`modules.jsonc`, the pieces every style shares** (network, Bluetooth, volume, clipboard, USB drives, printers, the bell, the tray, the emblem, the power buttons, CPU, memory, what's playing, the clock — `sway/waybar/modules.jsonc`, so a fix there reaches every style). hypeForge Classic's bar is the hand-made one in `sway/waybar/` (unchanged); another style's is `styles/<slug>/bar/`; a style without one leaves the bar alone. Backups and `undo` cover the bar like every other file. **A style starts the applets its bar needs** (`[layout] applets = ["taskbar"]` → `exec_always` lines in the theme's sway.conf; each applet keeps one copy, so reloads never double it). The Taskbar applet's own bench: `python3 scripts/taskbar-bench.py` (30 windows in a burst: alive, light, correct). **Switching under a running bar:** `python3 scripts/switch-bench.py` (every style in turn, two bar reloads each — F-58: Waybar quits on a missing `@import`, so the engine creates any missing imported stylesheet before it reloads). Bars find their includes with `${XDG_CONFIG_HOME:-$HOME/.config}/…`, so the same file works on the desktop and on the hidden bench. Whole-style bench: `python3 scripts/style-preview.py --style <s> --theme <t>`.

**The clipboard panel (D-101, 2026-10-17):** `applets/panels/views/clipboard.py`, on the panels' own look (every colour a panel role, every corner the style's). A head card (the clipboard glyph in `panel_tile_on`, **Clipboard** in the title size, "N kept" in `panel_text_dim`, **Clear all** flat; asking: **Empty it?** · **Yes** (danger) · **No**), the search box, and the list in one card (4 px padding): each copy a row, radius half the panel's (0 on square styles), 6 × 8 px padding, hover `panel_hover`, selected `panel_selected` / `panel_selected_text`; rows wear `panel_text` (never the GTK theme's: Mac OS 9 went grey-on-dark without it). A **40 × 40 badge** (`panel_tile`, the kind's Nerd Font glyph in `panel_tile_on` at 1.35 × the UI size; a colour copy is the colour itself; a picture its own middle square), two lines of the copy, a dim line (0.88 ×) with **● new** in `panel_tile_on`. Up to seven rows, then it scrolls (440 px wide). Preview with a fake history: `scripts/clipboard-preview.sh <style> <theme> <out.png>`.

# Quick Search — Win + Space

**What it does:** one search box, in the middle of the screen near the top. It opens empty; as you type,
it searches everything at once and shows each kind under its own heading, the **best match** first and
already picked, so **Enter** opens it straight away.

| Heading | What it finds | Enter does |
|---|---|---|
| **Apps & Settings** | your apps by name or by what they do, and hypeForge Settings' pages (Look, Screens, …) | opens the app on its own workspace, or Settings on that page |
| **Files** | files and folders in your home folder whose name holds what you typed | opens it; **Ctrl + Enter** opens the folder it's in |
| **Browser history** | pages you visited in Chrome, every profile (its name shows when you have more than one) | opens the page in that profile |
| **Clipboard** | things you copied | copies it again |
| **Do** | a web search for what you typed | searches the web in your browser |

**Two shortcuts inside it:**

- **`=`** then a sum calculates: `= (3+4)*12` shows **84**, and Enter copies the answer.
- **`>`** then a command runs it in a terminal: `> htop`.

**Keys:** ↑ ↓ move · **Tab** jumps to the next heading · Enter opens · Esc, or Win + Space again, closes.

**Private:** everything is searched on this computer. Chrome's history is read without changing it. Nothing
is sent anywhere unless you pick the web search. (Files: hidden folders and what projects mark as
"ignore" are skipped.)

The launcher of your style is **Win** on its own (page: Launcher).

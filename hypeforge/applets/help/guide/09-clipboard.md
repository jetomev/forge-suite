# Clipboard history (applet 9)

**What it does:** remembers the last 200 things you copied — text and pictures, screenshots
included — even after a restart. The **clipboard icon** on the bar counts the new ones.

## How to use it

- **Win + C**, or **click the clipboard icon**: your history, newest first. Pick a line of
  text to copy it again (then **Ctrl + V**); pick a 🖼 picture to open it in swappy and draw on
  it. Opening the list makes the number go away. Press again (or **Esc**) to close it.
- **Right click the icon** to empty the whole history (it asks first).
- **Passwords:** password managers mark what they copy as secret, and the history skips
  those.

## Settings

How many copies are kept: `~/.config/cliphist/config` (`max-items`).

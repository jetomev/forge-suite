# Screens

The first screen: your screens **drawn as they sit on your desk**, to scale — side by side, one above another, any layout. The star (★) marks the **main screen**: the one your workspaces start on (screen 1).

- **← →** pick a screen. Its details show underneath: what it shows, its size, its model, its workspace number.
- **All good** (green) means every screen is on and nothing overlaps. **Needs attention** (yellow) lists what is wrong — for example two screens covering the same space, or saved settings that differ from what is on screen.

Screens are numbered the way your workspaces count them, so **Win + 1** always starts on screen 1.

# Getting started

displayForge changes how your **screens** are set up: where each one sits, its resolution and refresh rate, how big things look, its rotation, and its brightness. It works on Sway, the KognogOS desktop.

## The three steps for a change

1. **Change** something in Settings or Arrange. Nothing happens on your screens yet; the bar at the bottom counts your changes, and each changed setting says **● changed · was: …**.
2. **Try it** with **F9**. The change appears on your screens and a window asks **"Keep these settings?"** with a countdown. Press **Keep it**, or do nothing: after 12 seconds it **goes back by itself**, so a screen that went black fixes itself.
3. **Save** with **F10**, to keep it after your next login. You see a review first (before → after), and a backup of the old file is made.

**Brightness is different:** it changes at once and the screens keep it themselves. There is nothing to try or save.

## Moving around

- **1 – 5** go to the screens: Screens, Settings, Arrange, Brightness, Identify (or the underlined letter).
- **Tab** goes to the next field or button, **Shift+Tab** back. **Enter** chooses, **Space** flips a switch.
- **?** lists every key. **M** opens this manual. **Q** quits.

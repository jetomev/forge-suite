# Arrange

Tell the computer where your screens sit, so the mouse moves from one to the next where you expect.

- **Tab** picks a screen. **← → ↑ ↓** move it next to its neighbour in that direction. The others **make room**, and edges snap together — no gaps, no overlaps.
- Or choose: **Put it** left of / right of / above / below **Of** another screen, and **Line up** by tops, centres or bottoms.

Any layout works: four in a row, three over three, one under another. Moving a screen **does not change its number** — the workspaces still start on the main one (★).

Try the new layout with **F9**, then save it with **F10**.

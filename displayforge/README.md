# 🖥 displayForge

> **Screen settings for the KognogOS desktop**, in the terminal: arrange your screens, pick resolution and refresh rate, scale, rotation, turn screens on and off, choose the main one, and set brightness — with a safety countdown that puts things back by itself if a change leaves you staring at a black screen. Part of the **[Forge Suite](../README.md)**.

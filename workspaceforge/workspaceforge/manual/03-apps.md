# Apps

Which apps open on which workspace, **however you start them**: the launcher, typing their name, Favorites, a terminal, or Steam starting a game. When one opens, your screens go with it to its workspace, except in the first 30 seconds after login, so apps that start by themselves don't move you.

## The two tables

- **On the left**, every app that is on no workspace yet: it opens wherever you are. Each row has a tick box, the app and its category. **Find** and the **category** list at the top narrow it down; a click on a heading sorts by it (again for the other way, ▲ ▼).
- **On the right**, your workspaces. Open one and it shows its apps; opening another closes the first.

## Sending apps across

1. Tick apps with **Space** (or a click).
2. **>>** (or the **>** key) sends the ticked apps on the left into the open workspace.
3. **<<** (or **<**) sends the ticked apps of the open workspace back to the left.

**Select All (a)** and **Deselect All (u)** tick only the rows you can see: the filtered list on the left, or the open workspace on the right.

One app, one workspace: an app sits either on the left or in exactly one workspace. To move one from Daily to Gaming, send it back from Daily, then send it to Gaming.

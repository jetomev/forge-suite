# Workspaces

Your workspaces down the left, with the Win key that reaches each one; **●** marks the one on screen now. On the right, the one you picked: its name, how many apps open there, whether it shares a screen, and what is open on it right now.

## The buttons on top

- **New (n)** — no window pops up: a "(new)" line appears in the list and the page becomes a blank form. Type a name, pick where it goes in the order, **Enter** creates it (**Esc** cancels). It starts with no apps.
- **Edit (e)** — the name becomes editable right on the page. **Enter** keeps it, **Esc** puts it back. After a rename a short yellow **⚠** note asks you to check its apps on the Apps page; it goes away when you pick another workspace.
- **Delete (d)** — this one asks first. If windows are open on it, you pick where they go (nothing is ever closed). The workspaces after it move up a number, and its apps go back to "opens where you are".
- **Move Up (+) · Move Down (-)** — over the list. Moving a workspace changes its Win number.

## The switch at the bottom

**Workspaces across all screens.** On: switching a workspace switches every screen together. Off: Sway's own way, where each screen switches by itself.

## How many

At least one always stays. Nine is the most for now: Win + 1 … 9 reach them.

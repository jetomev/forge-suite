# Getting started

workspaceForge sets up your **workspaces**: the desks that cover all your screens at once (Win + 4 takes every screen to Gaming). It edits the settings of hypeForge's Workspaces helper, which does the actual switching; your workspaces keep working with workspaceForge closed.

## The two pages

- **1 · Workspaces** — names and order: new, edit, delete, move up and down, and the main switch (all screens together, or each screen by itself).
- **2 · Apps** — which apps open on each workspace, however you start them.

**Sharing** (a screen keeping the same apps across workspaces) is being rethought and comes back in a later version. Whatever your settings file already says about it is kept when you save.

## Saving

Nothing changes until you save. The bar at the bottom counts your changes. **F10** shows a review (before → after), makes a backup of the file, and then everything happens **at once**: open windows go with their workspace, and the Workspaces helper reads the new settings. No logout.

**Discard Changes** on the bottom bar puts everything back as it is saved.

## Moving around

- **1 – 3**, or **Ctrl** + the underlined letter: Workspaces, Apps, Help.
- **Tab / Shift + Tab**: next / previous field, table or button.
- **?**: every key. **M**: this manual. **Q**: quit (it asks first if something isn't saved).

# Sharing

Workspaces down, screens across, and a switch in every cell: **○ Own** or **● Shared**.

Switch a screen to **Shared** in the workspaces that should keep the same apps on it. Whatever is on that screen (your chat, your music) then stays put when you switch between those workspaces, while the other screens change.

- **Arrow keys** move between the switches; **Space** or **Enter** flips one.
- One cell alone shares nothing: it takes two or more in a column.
- Each screen has at most one shared group.
- A new window can still land on a shared screen (window placement decides where); it then shows in every workspace of that group, as sharing means.
- **Save (F10)** at the bottom, or **Undo Changes (Esc)** to put the switches back as saved.

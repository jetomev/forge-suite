# Getting started

nightForge looks after the **night light**: after sunset your screens turn a little warmer (less blue), and at sunrise they go back to normal. Screenshots and what you share are not affected.

## Three ways in

- **The tray icon** on the bar: a muted ☀ by day, a mustard ☾ while warm, a dim ○ when off. A click shows its menu: Automatic · Warm Now · Daylight Now · Turn Off / Turn On · Open nightForge.
- **This app**: hypeForge Settings → Night light, or the launcher.
- **A terminal**: `nightforge status` says what it is doing now.

## What acts at once, and what waits for Save

- **At once:** the on/off switch (saved for every login too) and **Right now**.
- **With Save (F10):** the evening warmth and the schedule. You see a review first; then the night light restarts with them.

## Moving around

**1 – 3** or **Ctrl** + the underlined letter: Night Light, Schedule, Help. **?** every key, **M** this manual, **Q** quit (it asks if something isn't saved).

# Night Light

- **Right now** box: warm or daylight, and when it changes next.
- **Night light On / Off (o):** off means no warm colours, now and at every login, until you switch it on again.
- **Right now:** **Automatic** follows the schedule. **Warm Now** and **Daylight Now** hold until you pick Automatic again, or log out — handy for a late movie, or for checking colours in a photo.
- **Evening warmth:** how warm the evening gets, in kelvin (lower is warmer): 3000 very warm · 3500 warm · 4000 gentle · 4500 light · 5000 just a touch. Or type any number from 1000 to 6400.
- **Preview (p):** shows a warmth on your screens for 10 seconds, then goes back by itself. **Keep** it, then **F10** saves it.
- **Daytime** is the screens' own white (6500 K); there is nothing to set.

# Wallpaper

The picture behind your windows. **Every screen shows the same one.**

## Where it comes from

- **The theme's own** — each theme has its own picture, drawn in the colours you picked, so it changes when you change the colours. **Linux rice** shows the KognogOS background; the other themes each have a topic (Windows 11 ribbons, KDE shards, COSMIC a planet and stars, macOS mountains, Mac OS 9 sky and clouds, hypeForge Classic a circuit board).
- **KognogOS · Tier Aware** and **KognogOS · Arch Distro** — our backgrounds, in the colours you picked, whatever the theme.
- **My pictures** — one of your own pictures from the **Folder** (`~/Pictures/Wallpapers` unless you type another and press Enter). If the folder isn't there yet, **Make Folder (n)** makes it; then put your pictures in it (PNG, JPEG, WebP…). Your picture stays when you change the theme or colours.
- **Solid colour** — no picture, just the theme's own colour.

## How it lies on the screen

- **Fill** — covers the whole screen; the edges of the picture are cropped. The usual choice.
- **Fit** — the whole picture, with the theme's colour around it.
- **Stretch** — the whole picture, pulled to the screen's shape.
- **Center** — the picture at its real size, in the middle.
- **Tile** — the picture repeated across the screen.

## The preview

Under the choices, a small picture of how your screens will look, drawn with the terminal's own characters: rough, but enough to tell one picture from another. The first preview of a theme's picture can take a few seconds while it's drawn.

**Apply (F10)** in the bar at the bottom sets it (with a theme and colours you picked on the Theme Picker, if any). **Undo Last Apply (u)** brings back what you had. The lock screen follows the wallpaper by itself.

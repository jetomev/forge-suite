# Wallpaper

The picture behind your windows. **Every screen shows the same one.**

## Source

Where the pictures come from: a folder. The line shows the one in use. **Browse (b)** opens a small window like a file manager: **the folders as a tree on the left**, and **the pictures of the folder under the cursor on the right**. The tree starts with **The theme's pictures** (the ones hypeForge draws for you, see below), **Home** (open by default) and the **KognogOS wallpapers** the system ships. **↑ ↓** move, **→** or **Enter** opens a folder, **←** closes it or goes up a level, **Tab** jumps to the pictures and **Enter** on one picks it. **Use This Folder (u)** takes the folder under the cursor; **Esc** leaves things as they were. The window opens on the folder in use. A folder you pick shows its first picture right away.

If the folder isn't there yet, **Make Folder (n)** makes it; then put your pictures in it (PNG, JPEG, WebP…) and they show up.

## Images

Every picture in the Source, by name. Move with the arrows and press **Enter** to pick one: it shows below, and **Apply Background (F10)** lights up. **✓** marks the picture on screen now, **●** the one you picked.

**The theme's pictures** are four, and on Linux rice ten:

- **The theme's own** — each theme has its own picture, drawn in the colours you picked, so it changes when you change the colours. **Linux rice** shows the KognogOS background; the other themes each have a topic (Windows 11 ribbons, KDE shards, COSMIC a planet and stars, macOS mountains, Mac OS 9 sky and clouds, hypeForge Classic a circuit board).
- **KognogOS · Tier Aware** and **KognogOS · Arch Distro** — our backgrounds, in the colours you picked, whatever the theme.
- **On Linux rice only, the other themes' drawn pictures** — Circuit board, Mountains, Planet and stars, Ribbons, Shards, Sky and clouds, each drawn in the colours you picked. Picking one changes only the wallpaper: the theme stays Linux rice.
- **Solid colour** — no picture, just the theme's own colour.

A picture of your own stays when you change the theme or colours.

## Fit Style

- **Fill the Screen** — covers the whole screen; the edges of the picture are cropped. The usual choice.
- **Whole Picture** — all of the picture shows, with the theme's colour around it.
- **Stretched** — the whole picture, pulled to the screen's shape.
- **Real Size** — the picture at its real size, in the middle.
- **Repeated** — the picture repeated across the screen.

## The preview

Under the list, a picture of how your screens will look. On hypeForge it's the real picture, at full quality, laid over the window — inside hypeForge Settings too; it steps away while a list or the Browse window is open, another window is in use, or Settings shows another page. In a plain text console it's drawn with the terminal's own characters instead: rough, but enough to tell one picture from another. The first preview of a theme's picture can take a few seconds while it's drawn.

**Apply Background (F10)** sets it. **Undo Last Apply (u)** brings back what you had. The lock screen follows the wallpaper by itself.

If you also picked a theme or colours on the Theme Picker, the bar at the bottom says so; F10 applies all of it at once.

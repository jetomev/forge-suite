# Getting started

themeForge sets **the look of your desktop**: which **theme** hypeForge wears, and in which **colours**.

## Theme Picker

- **Theme** — how the desktop is laid out: where the bar sits, what's on it, the dock, the pop-ups. Seven to pick from, each described under its drop-down; **Linux rice** is KognogOS's own.
- **Color** — the colours it all wears: 25 colour themes, light and dark. The strip under the drop-down shows the one you picked: its grays, its colour, its accent and its text. Every colour theme brings its own wallpaper.

Pick both, then **Apply (F10)**: the bar, the windows, the pop-ups and lists, the notifications, the lock screen, the terminal and the wallpaper all switch at once. The bar restarts for a moment.

## Going back

**Undo Last Apply (u)** brings back the look from before the last apply. Every apply keeps a backup of what it replaced (the last 20).

Until you apply, nothing changes: the bar at the bottom says what is picked but not applied, and **Put Back** returns the drop-downs to the look in use.

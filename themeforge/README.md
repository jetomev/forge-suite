# themeForge

**The look of your desktop.** Pick hypeForge's **theme** (how the desktop is laid out: the bar, the dock, the pop-ups) and its **colours** (25 colour themes, light and dark, each with its own wallpaper), and apply them in one step: the bar, the windows, the pop-ups and lists, the notifications, the lock screen and the terminal all follow. One step back is always there. A terminal app, and the Look page of hypeForge Settings.

> 🖥 **Where it runs:** **any Linux distribution** · **hypeForge on Sway** (it switches hypeForge's look) · **any terminal, even a plain text console**.
